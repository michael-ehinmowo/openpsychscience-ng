Research from Nigeria, done in the open.

OPSN is a community of students, early-career researchers, clinicians and academics working to make psychological science in Nigeria and across Africa transparent, reproducible, and easier for others to build on.

What we do

Journal Club

We read and discuss the papers that shaped the credibility movement in psychology, and work out what they mean for research done here. Sessions are open to anyone.

Workshops and training

Practical sessions on the tools of open research — preregistration, version control, reproducible analysis, and sharing data responsibly.

Resources

Guides, templates and reading lists, chosen for researchers working with the constraints and realities of Nigerian institutions.

Community

A place for students and early-career researchers to find collaborators, ask questions, and get feedback on work in progress.

Why it matters here

Psychological science is built overwhelmingly on evidence from a narrow slice of the world. African samples, African questions and African researchers are underrepresented in the literature — and when the work does appear, it is often hard to check, hard to repeat, and hard to build on.

Open practices are how that changes. They make research from this region easier to trust, easier to cite, and easier for the next student to learn from. That is the work OPSN exists to support.

Get involved

You do not need to be an expert in open science to take part — most of our members are learning it alongside their own research. Come to a Journal Club session, suggest a resource, or tell us what you would find useful.
